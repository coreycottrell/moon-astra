Read the example's assumptions and calculation

Both mines can excavate 100 raw units/hour; useful recovery is 80% of ore grade. Nearby ground has 12% grade, a 250 m one-way route and one hauler. Each hauler carries 12 units at 2 m/s, with six minutes total handling per round trip. The distant mine uses the selected route and hauler count.

Concentrating reduces shipped mass to half the raw feed, leaves a local residue, and caps processing at 60 raw units/hour. Maximum useful recovery in this example is below that shipped mass. It adds equipment and energy demand that this comparison does not price.

Round trip minutes = 6 + (2 × distance / 2) / 60
Haul units/hour = haulers × 12 × 60 / round trip minutes
Useful output = min(excavation, processing, raw-equivalent hauling) × grade × 0.8

This is a teaching model with invented quantities. It omits traffic, utility limits, storage queues, wear, construction cost and shared crew duties; game planning must account for those too. It reads no live game state and issues no commands.
