Private backups go to `/var/backups/moon-astra-v2/<UTC>/`; latest.json records paths, source versions, counts and hashes. No automatic retention deletion is configured. Copy snapshots off-host and monitor storage. The initial pair was copied to the tower and SHA/integrity checked.
