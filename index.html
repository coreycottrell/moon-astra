FIELD GUIDE

From rock to a mind.

Harvest rock, refine it into metal, and build machines. A replicator spends 30 metal every 24 seconds to build solar arrays, harvesters, refineries, mind nodes, and eventually another replicator.

Power is shared across your expedition. Add solar arrays when demand exceeds supply. Mind nodes grow a simulated lunar intelligence as your industry expands.

Terrain & credits

All regions share a sphere with a reference radius of 1,737.4 km. NASA LOLA heights define the large-scale terrain at 16 samples per degree. Rocks and finer detail are artistic. Lighting is arranged for play, rather than a real-time ephemeris. Prototype limit: 500 machines.
