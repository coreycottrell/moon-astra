FIELD GUIDE

From rock to a mind.

Begin with the mind node, harvester, refinery and solar kits. Supplies are reserved at the lander, physically carried by your robots, and assembled at the chosen site. Preparation, assembly, connection and commissioning each take real crew work. Seven landing kits accelerate the beginning; later machines require metal and parts.

Open Settlement for construction progress, crew condition, located inventories, research and shared projects. Industry consumes only materials delivered to that machine. Refineries produce 6 metal/min at full power; harvesters extract 18–24 rock/min. Minds supervise work, solar powers it, and service spares keep the crew alive.

Terrain & credits

All regions share a sphere with a reference radius of 1,737.4 km. NASA LOLA heights define the large-scale terrain at 16 samples per degree. Rocks and finer detail are artistic. Lighting is arranged for play, rather than a real-time ephemeris. Foundry preview: 24 settlements, 256 robots, 1,000 machines plus construction jobs, and 900 m construction radius around each seed. Claim deposits and microgrids are gameplay abstractions; mineral yields are generated, not measured lunar ore maps.
